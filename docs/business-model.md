# Business Model: Upholstery Workshop Scheduling and Logistics Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7534`
- ISCO-08: `7534`
- Occupation: Upholsterers and Related Workers
- Social impact: worker-safety, product-quality, craft-continuity

## Customer

- upholstery workshop operators / furniture-restoration contractors
- independent upholstery crews / trim shops

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- job/commission/progress-record logging
- padding/fabric/frame-materials supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to shop safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of an upholstery-execution decision (e.g.
  deciding an upholstery job is finished), ever
- no declaration of a workshop-safety-clearance decision, ever
- no override of a shop safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (hand-tool hazard, material-handling
  hazard, equipment condition) always route to human sign-off,
  regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
